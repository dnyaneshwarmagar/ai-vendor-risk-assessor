# Third-Party Risk Policy

Document ID: NM-TPR-POL · Version 2026.1 · Effective 1 February 2026 · Owner: Procurement with Information Security

## 1. Scope

### TPR-1.1 When a review is required
Any SaaS or AI tool that will process Nimbu Mart data (T1–T3) or that more than five employees will use must complete the vendor risk assessment process in PROC-1.1 before production use.

### TPR-1.2 Sub-processors
Vendors must disclose sub-processors that touch customer content. Model providers count as sub-processors under AI-3.4.

## 2. Ongoing monitoring

### TPR-2.1 Annual re-review
Approved T2 and T3 vendors are re-reviewed at least annually, or sooner after a material incident, ownership change, or region change.
