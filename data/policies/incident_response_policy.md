# Vendor Incident Response Policy

Document ID: NM-IR-POL · Version 2026.1 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: Head of Information Security

## 1. Notification

### IR-1.1 Vendor breach notification
A vendor that processes Nimbu Mart data must notify the Head of Information Security within **72 hours** of confirming a security incident that affects or may affect our data. The notice must include scope, data types, and containment status.

### IR-1.2 Internal escalation
On receipt of a vendor incident notice, Information Security opens an IR ticket and informs the DPO when personal data is involved.

## 2. Evidence

### IR-2.1 Post-mortem requirement
For any major vendor incident in the prior 24 months, a written post-mortem (or equivalent RCA) must be on file before T3 approval (see also SEC-9.3).
